# Sources for history and places

Reference works to check the game's history, places and dates against (`content/history.json`, `content/drive.json`,
`content/walk.json`, `content/storybook.json`). An item in `NEEDS_REVIEW.md` is closed only when a source here (or
another named source) confirms it; write the source and page beside the item when it is checked.

Nothing here is copied into the game: claims are checked against these works and written in our own words.

## Encyclopaedias of Palestine

| Work | What it is | Access | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Palestinica"**: *Encyclopaedia Palestinnica*, Dr. Mohammad Hashem Ghosheh (a historian of Jerusalem) | Listed by booksellers as 24 volumes on Palestine in the Ottoman period, pictorial and documentary, English text with facsimiles mostly in Arabic. Its photographs and documents would help with the "then" views (about 1910) and the khans on the road | Printed volumes only; not found online. Claude cannot read it | Added 19 November 2026 at the owner's request. **To confirm with the owner that this is the work meant**, and whether the family has a copy (photographs of the relevant pages could then be checked) |
| *Al-Mawsu'a al-Filastiniyya* (الموسوعة الفلسطينية), Damascus | The large Arabic Palestinian Encyclopedia, with entries on towns, villages and sites | Printed; no working official site found (encyclopedia.ps does not resolve) | Possibly the work meant; to confirm with the owner |
| *Encyclopedia of the Palestinians*, ed. Philip Mattar (Facts on File; 3rd ed. with Michael R. Fischbach, 2019) | One volume in English on Palestinian history and society, late Ottoman period to today | Printed | Not yet used |
| *Interactive Encyclopedia of the Palestine Question* (PalQuest), Institute for Palestine Studies and the Palestinian Museum | Bilingual (Arabic and English) online: events, people, places, documents, mostly from the late Ottoman period on | Online: https://www.palquest.org/en | Not yet used |
