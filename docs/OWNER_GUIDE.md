## PART C — YOUR GUIDE

### Set your expectations
- Fable can build you a game that is **genuinely beautiful for the browser**, in a stylised-realistic look. A literal Forza or Need for Speed experience depends on hundreds of artists and hand-made 3D models. To get closer later, keep the code that Fable writes and add: (a) commissioned 3D models of the landmarks and cars, (b) recorded voice acting, and (c) possibly a port to **Unreal Engine 5 or Unity**. The data-driven content design in this prompt makes that port much easier.
- The phased approach matters. If you ask for "the whole game" in one go, you will get a wide but shallow and buggy result. A polished 10-minute vertical slice is worth more than a rough 10-hour game.

### Decide these before you start (or let Fable ask you)
1. **Platform:** is the browser and tablets OK, or do you need a PC, console or mobile app store release?
2. **Era:** is a fixed 1910 setting right, or do you want a "timeless Ottoman" world? The 1910 setting makes cars plausible and adds the Hejaz Railway.
3. **Main characters:** names, and whether players choose a boy or a girl.
4. **Music policy:** nasheed and percussion only, or instruments allowed?
5. **Makkah and Madinah:** include them as respectful, drive-to-the-outskirts, on-foot-only visits, or leave them out entirely?
6. **Scholarly review:** who will check `religious_texts.json` and `NEEDS_REVIEW.md` before release? This is strongly recommended.
7. **Monetisation:** free, paid once, or for a school or education market? The prompt bans loot boxes and paywalls on purpose.

### After each phase, check
- [ ] Does it run at 60 fps on your own laptop, and on a tablet?
- [ ] Do the screenshots at sunset genuinely make you say "wow"? If not, ask for the art-direction pass.
- [ ] Can your child (or a child you know) play 10 minutes without help?
- [ ] Is every religious text and historical fact accurate? (Check against `religious_texts.json` and `NEEDS_REVIEW.md`.)
- [ ] Does the Arabic text render correctly from right to left, with connected letters?
- [ ] Commit or back up the project before you start the next phase.
