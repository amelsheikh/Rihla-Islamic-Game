Rihla: Roads Without Borders - prototype (19 November 2026)
===========================================================

This is a test build for the family. It is not finished, and some facts in it are
still waiting to be checked.

Please do not share this folder or the zip outside the family. It contains the
project's private key for the 3D map of Al-Quds today.


HOW TO START
------------
Double-click one of these:

  1 - Walk in Al-Quds.bat     Start at Bab al-Asbat and walk with Jiddo to
                               al-Masjid al-Aqsa (about 10 minutes).
  2 - Drive from Al-Quds.bat  Start on the road from Al-Quds down to Ariha.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info", then "Run anyway". This appears because the game is not signed.

On the first screen, choose Yusuf or Maryam. Each start begins a fresh game.


WALKING IN AL-QUDS
------------------
  W A S D          walk
  Shift            run
  Right mouse      look around
  V                first person / behind the child
  E                look closely at a clue
  T                Time Lens: about 1910 / today (after the first reveal)
  C                choose Yusuf or Maryam
  G                back to the car
  L                English / Arabic
  Esc              close a page or the menu

Follow Jiddo. Find the three clues: the lions on Bab al-Asbat, the tiles of the
Dome of the Rock, and the dome of the Qibli Mosque.


DRIVING
-------
  Arrow keys or W A S D   drive
  Space                   handbrake
  E                       get out and walk (when stopped)
  P                       Jiddo drives
  C                       fly over Al-Quds today
  J                       journal
  L                       English / Arabic
  Esc                     menu


WHAT TO LOOK FOR WHEN YOU TEST
------------------------------
- Does it run smoothly on your laptop?
- Does it feel like walking in the Old City? What looks wrong or missing?
- Is anything confusing for the children?
- Is any text, Arabic or English, wrong?

Write down what you notice and tell Claude in the next session.
