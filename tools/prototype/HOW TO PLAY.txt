Rihla: Roads Without Borders - prototype (19 November 2026)
===========================================================

This is a test build for the family. It is not finished, and some facts in it are
still waiting to be checked.

Please do not share this folder or the zip outside the family. It contains the
project's private key for the 3D map of Al-Quds today.


HOW TO START
------------
Double-click one of these:

  1 - Start the story.bat     The game from the beginning: an evening at
                               Jiddo's house, his old book of places, the walk
                               to al-Masjid al-Aqsa at dawn, Jiddo's chest and
                               Zaynab's riddle, then the road to Ariha.
  2 - Walk in Al-Quds.bat     Straight to the walk from Bab al-Asbat to
                               al-Masjid al-Aqsa (about 10 minutes).
  3 - Drive from Al-Quds.bat  Straight to the road from Al-Quds down to Ariha.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click
"More info", then "Run anyway". This appears because the game is not signed.

Each start begins a fresh game. At the end of Jiddo's story you choose who
walks with him: Yusuf or Maryam.


THE STORY AT JIDDO'S HOUSE
--------------------------
  Space or Enter   turn the page of the book (or the arrow keys, or the button)
  L                English / Arabic
  Esc              skip the story


WALKING IN AL-QUDS
------------------
  W A S D          walk
  Shift            run
  Right mouse      look around
  V                first person / behind the child
  E                look closely at a clue
  T                Time Lens: about 1910 / today (after the first reveal)
  C                choose Yusuf or Maryam
  G                back to the car
  L                English / Arabic
  Esc              close a page or the menu

The box at the top of the screen always says what to do next. Golden arrows on
the ground show the way, and a golden arrow floats over Jiddo or over the next
clue. Each clue waits in a small glowing box with red embroidery (tatreez),
floating over a golden ring: walk into the ring to open it. Find the three
clues: the lions on Bab al-Asbat, the tiles of the Dome of the Rock, and the
dome of the Qibli Mosque. Then Jiddo takes you home.


BACK HOME: JIDDO'S CHEST
------------------------
Jiddo opens the old family chest and you read the first page of Zaynab, your
grandmother from long ago. Press Space or the button for her riddle, then
choose on the map where she went next. If you choose wrong, Jiddo gives a
hint. Then the drive down to Ariha begins.


DRIVING
-------
  Arrow keys or W A S D   drive
  Space                   handbrake
  E                       get out and walk (when stopped)
  P                       Jiddo drives
  C                       fly over Al-Quds today
  J                       journal
  L                       English / Arabic
  Esc                     menu
  R                       back onto the road

The box at the top says where to drive next: first stop by the old ruin of Khan
al-Ahmar, where Jiddo tells you what a khan was; then the golden scroll at Khan
al-Hatruri (stop beside it to read the page); then down to where the road goes
below the level of the sea, and on to Ariha. One soft golden arrow floats over
the road ahead and turns with it; it glows brighter when you stop or go the
wrong way. On the way Jiddo talks about the land, and a shepherd crosses the
road with his flock: stop and wait for them.

In Ariha, park by the golden light at the spring and press E to get out. With
Jiddo beside you, find three glowing boxes: by the spring, at the old tower in
Tell al-Sultan, and under the date palms. Then walk to Umm Khalil's fruit stall:
help her put the ripe brown dates in her basket, answer her question, and she
gives you Zaynab's second page and its riddle.


WHAT TO LOOK FOR WHEN YOU TEST
------------------------------
- Does it run smoothly on your laptop?
- Does it feel like walking in the Old City? What looks wrong or missing?
- Is anything confusing for the children?
- Do the Qibli Mosque and the Dome of the Rock look right? What is different?
- Are the sounds calm enough? Too loud, too quiet?
- Is any text, Arabic or English, wrong?

Write down what you notice and tell Claude in the next session.
